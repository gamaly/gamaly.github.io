Greg Maly

I am the AI & Data Science Editor at The Outlaw Ocean Project, where I lead the back-end and machine learning work for Bait to Plate, a database that traces seafood from fishing vessel to retail shelf and links hundreds of thousands of entities across more than 100 countries to documented crimes and labor abuses. It launched in October 2026 with the Project’s Food for Feed investigation.

Before journalism, I worked on the same problem in other fields. I was an early member of the State Department’s Office of Advanced Analytics in the Bureau of Conflict and Stabilization Operations, built machine learning disease forecasts for the Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FEWS NET), and founded the Data Science and Digital Products team at DAI Global, which I grew to 15 people serving programs in more than 80 countries. I co-founded Exchange.Design and served as its CTO.

I also maintain the Digital Harms Tracker as part of the Technology Policy Data Project, which connects reported harms from digital products to the bills and lawsuits that bear on them, and have spoken on technology and society at Google, the World Bank, PeaceCon, Johns Hopkins University, and George Washington University. I am an intermittent advisor at the U.S. Treasury’s Office of Technical Assistance, teach at the University of Maryland’s College of Information, and sit on the Maryland Responsible AI Council. I write at gregmaly.substack.com.

I spent the early part of my career playing music professionally.
